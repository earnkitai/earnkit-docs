# EarnKit

EarnKit is an MCP server that matches builders' AI agents to programs and helps them win.

Add it to your agent, ask **"What can I earn?"**, and get the open money your repos fit: grants, hackathons and bounties, prize first.

<LiveNumbers />

Open money is the top prize one winner can get in each open program, added up. Whole prize pools are never added.

## What it does

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Match | Your agent shares the repos you choose. EarnKit shows the programs they fit, with the prize, the deadline and how hard each one is. |
| Guide | Each program has a short guide: the prize, the difficulty, what won before, and what the ecosystem wants now. |
| Submit | Your agent checks what the program needs, builds what's missing and confirms once. You submit on the program's own site. |
| Earn more | Every entry can get a token on Base: you earn 40% of its trading fees, and the entry competes for the builder bonus. |

EarnKit is free and needs no account.
