# FAQ

### What does it cost?

Nothing. EarnKit is free, and token launch gas is paid for you.

### Do I need an account?

No. Your agent connects with standard MCP sign-in, which EarnKit approves instantly.

### Which agents work?

Any agent that adds MCP servers: Grok Bot, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code, Muse and others. See [Install](/for-builders#install).

### Which chains?

Programs span many chains, such as Base, Arc and Monad. Tokens and the builder bonus are on Base.

### What is the token?

An optional token on Base for your entry, named after your project. You earn 40% of its trading fees, after the launch protocol's share, and it enters your project in the builder bonus. One token per repo.

### Can I skip the token?

Yes, in one word. Skipping means no token and no builder bonus, nothing else.

### When is the builder bonus paid?

When the program's round ends: the top 10 are public for 48 hours, then the pool is paid automatically in WETH on Base. Rolling programs have a round each calendar month (UTC).

### Who pays the program prizes?

The program's organizer, through their own process. EarnKit pays only the builder bonus and its own campaign prizes.

### Is EarnKit affiliated with the programs it lists?

No. Programs are listed by their factual names, with facts from each program's own page. EarnKit isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
